macOS: bring the iOS in-note AI editing to the Mac Notes editor — one-tap Edit chips (Shorten by 20%, Make it bullets, Summarize, Turn into email, Extract action items, More formal, Fix grammar) plus a free-form command field, with an inline red/green diff and Undo/Keep before anything is committed. Your most-used commands float to the front, and the command list is shared with iOS so both platforms stay in sync.
